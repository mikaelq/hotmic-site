Privacy policy

Before your next meeting

A few good questions.

Which AirPods and Macs work?

You’ll need macOS 15 or later and AirPods (3rd generation or later) or AirPods Pro. If you’re on macOS 27, check compatibility during the trial.

Do I need the browser extension?

Yes, to sync Google Meet’s own mute button through the browser bridge. The setup guide helps you connect it in Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc. Without that connection, HotMic can use device-level mute instead.

What about Zoom, FaceTime and Teams?

Zoom 6.4+, FaceTime and the native Teams app already support the stem gesture, so HotMic leaves them alone. Teams on the web is in beta. In other apps, HotMic can mute your Mac’s mics, but the app’s mute indicator may not change.

What happens after 14 days?

Device mute, the mute shortcut, Go to meeting and Join Meet muted stay free. Pro adds Meet mute sync, Stealth Mute and push-to-talk, with a one-time purchase.

Does HotMic record my conversations?

No. Your audio is never recorded, stored or sent. HotMic runs on your Mac, with no analytics or trackers. Read the privacy policy.
