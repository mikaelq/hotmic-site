Privacy policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This is the whole policy. It covers the HotMic app, its browser extension and helper components, and this website.

Your audio stays on your Mac

To receive the AirPods mute gesture from macOS, HotMic runs an audio unit that the system treats as handling call audio. Every audio sample that unit receives is rendered and immediately discarded, locally, on your Mac. Your audio is never recorded, never stored, and never transmitted off your device by HotMic. There is no code path that could send it anywhere.

The one network request the app makes

HotMic periodically contacts its update server to check for new versions (using the Sparkle update framework). Like any web request, this transmits your IP address, along with the app version, so the server can answer "is there something newer?". That is the app's only network activity. License activation is verified locally on your Mac.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Paddle as merchant of record. When you buy a license, Paddle — not us — collects and processes your payment details under Paddle's privacy policy. We receive from Paddle what is needed to issue your license key, such as your email address and the tier you purchased.

No analytics, anywhere

The app contains no analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting that phones home. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from third-party servers.
