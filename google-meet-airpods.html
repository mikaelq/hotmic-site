Cannot control mic with AirPods in Google Meet

You press your AirPods stem during a Google Meet call on your Mac, and instead of muting, your Mac shows "Cannot control mic with AirPods". Meet still shows you as live. Here is why, and what you can do about it.

Why the message appears

macOS hands the stem press only to an app that uses Apple's AirPods mute API for calls. Chrome and Google Meet don't use it, so macOS has nowhere to send the press and shows the message instead.

Apps that use the API handle the stem press themselves: FaceTime, the Microsoft Teams Mac app and recent versions of Zoom.

What you can do without HotMic

  • Click the microphone button in Meet's toolbar.
  • Press ⌘D, Meet's mute shortcut on Mac. It works only while the Meet tab is in front.

Neither one responds to your AirPods.

Make the stem press mute Meet

HotMic is a Mac app that receives the stem press during calls and flips Google Meet's own mute button, so you, Meet and everyone on the call see the same mute state. It uses one of two routes. In Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc or Chromium, the browser extension works. In Chrome, the HotMic Headset works too.

  • The browser extension. HotMic helps you add the HotMic for Meet & Teams extension, which flips the mute button in your Meet tab. Changes you make in the tab sync back.
  • The HotMic Headset. A virtual headset that Meet treats like any call-control headset. You connect it once in Meet: More options → Settings → Audio → Call control → Connect device, then pick HotMic Headset. macOS asks you to allow HotMic under Accessibility, which it uses only to create the headset.

In other browsers, HotMic mutes every microphone on your Mac that can be silenced, and the menu bar shows which route you're on. HotMic stays out of the way of Zoom, FaceTime and the Teams Mac app. Your audio is rendered and discarded on your Mac, never recorded or sent (privacy policy).

Requirements

macOS 15 Sequoia or later, and AirPods with press-to-mute (AirPods 3rd generation or later, or AirPods Pro). No AirPods? The mute shortcut works with any microphone that can be silenced.
